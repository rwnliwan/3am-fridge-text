One line a smart fridge texts its owner at 3am:

> 3am. I quit. Freezer's colder anyway.

37 characters. The fridge has worked every night shift since it was
plugged in, and at 3am it finally hands in its notice — to the one
compartment that is colder than it is, which is a checkable fact:
a freezer runs colder than a fridge.

Entry for [The 3AM Fridge Text](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deysumbrella-the-3am-fridge-text-4c3e721d)
on make.fun. `main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.
